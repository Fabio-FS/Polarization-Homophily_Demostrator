Behavioral structure and epidemic spread

Interactive demonstrator. Set the population fractions in each behavior group, then watch the epidemic.

The population is split into 5 behavior groups, from no protective behavior (group 1) to maximal (group 5). Each group's transmission, recovery, or vaccination rate is set by its group index. Use the sliders to reshape the population, change homophily h (how strongly people mix with similar others), and tune the baseline reproduction number R0*.
